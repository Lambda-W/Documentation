# FAQ & Troubleshooting

<br>
### I need help, where do I get it?
> Join us on Discord, and ask your question in the '*Asset Bookmarks*' channel. We'll try our best to help you out. [https://discord.com/invite/bxgtNY8](https://discord.com/invite/bxgtNY8){:target="_blank"}

<br>
### What platforms is this plugin for?
> Asset Bookmarks is an editor only tool, if your machine can open the Unreal Engine editor, the tool will work just fine. It does not affect your packaged game at all.
